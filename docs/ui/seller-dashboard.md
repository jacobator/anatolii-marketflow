# Seller Dashboard

The Seller Dashboard is your central command center for managing your shop on the Online Marketplace. It provides real-time insights into your sales performance and tools to manage your business operations.

## Viewing Analytics

The dashboard landing page displays a summary of your shop's performance. You can track key metrics such as:

- **Total Sales**: Your gross revenue over the selected period.
- **Page Views**: How many times customers have viewed your listings.
- **Conversion Rate**: The percentage of views that resulted in a purchase.

Use the date picker at the top of the page to filter data by day, week, or month to identify sales trends and peak shopping times.

## Managing Your Listings

The **Listings** tab allows you to control your catalog without writing a single line of code.

- **Edit Products**: Update prices, descriptions, and photos instantly.
- **Inventory Tracking**: Monitor stock levels and receive notifications when items are running low.
- **Bulk Actions**: Select multiple items to toggle their visibility or apply discounts across a category.

For more detailed instructions on catalog management, see [Managing Inventory](/docs/ui/managing-inventory).

## Communicating with Buyers

Effective communication is key to maintaining a high seller rating. The **Messages** section handles all interactions with your customers.

- **Inquiry Management**: Reply to questions about product specifications or shipping times.
- **Order Updates**: Send personalized notes to buyers once their order has been shipped.
- **Dispute Resolution**: Address customer concerns directly to resolve issues before they escalate.

{% admonition type="info" %}
Prompt responses (within 24 hours) are highlighted on your public profile and can improve your search ranking in the marketplace.
{% /admonition %}
