# Buyer Experience

The Online Marketplace provides a seamless shopping experience, from discovering new items to tracking them all the way to your doorstep.

## Searching for Products

Finding the right item is easy using the global search bar at the top of every page. You can search by keywords, brand, or category.

- **Filters:** Once you've searched, use the sidebar to filter results by price range, star rating, and shipping speed.
- **Sorting:** Sort results by "Best Match," "Price: Low to High," or "Newest Arrivals" to find exactly what you need.

## The Checkout Flow

When you're ready to buy, the checkout process is designed to be secure and straightforward.

1. **Cart:** Review your items and adjust quantities.
2. **Shipping:** Select a saved address or enter a new one.
3. **Payment:** Choose from several secure payment methods, including credit cards and digital wallets.
4. **Review:** Double-check your order total and shipping details before clicking "Place Order."

{% admonition type="info" %}
Your payment information is encrypted and never stored directly on our servers.
{% /admonition %}

## Tracking Your Orders

After a purchase, you can monitor its progress directly from your profile.

Navigate to **My Account > Order History** to see a list of your recent purchases. Each order displays its current status:
- **Pending:** The seller is preparing your order.
- **Shipped:** The package is with the carrier; a tracking link will be provided.
- **Delivered:** The package has arrived at its destination.

If you have questions about a specific order, you can use the "Contact Seller" button on the order details page.
